# Contributors

One Press is built and maintained by the people listed here. Thank you to
everyone who has contributed.

## Maintainers

- **darkwebber** ([@darkwebber](https://github.com/darkwebber)) — creator and
  maintainer: game design, engine and simulation code, station chapters, world
  art direction, HUD, and audio integration.

## How to be listed

This list is updated as contributions land. To be added:

1. Open a pull request with a focused change (a fix, a station, a doc, a test).
2. Make sure your commits use a name and email, or ask to be credited with a
   chosen name and link.

Everyone whose work is merged is welcome here, whatever the size of the change.

## Upstream projects

These are not code contributors to this repository, but the game depends on
their work and credits them with thanks:

- [Three.js](https://threejs.org/) — MIT — 3D engine and post-processing.
- **Paper Mono** by [Paper Design](https://github.com/paper-design/paper-mono) —
  SIL OFL 1.1 — the game's typeface.
- [Strudel](https://strudel.cc/) (`@strudel/web`) — AGPL-3.0-or-later — the
